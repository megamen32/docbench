# Russian policy-rule seed v1.0

**12 self-authored Russian policy documents** for the `rule_extraction`
benchmark. Version `ru-policy-seed-v1.0`, published 2026-08-18.

This is a compact, deterministic regression/evaluation seed, not a claim of
coverage of Russian law. It exercises eligibility, limits, required documents,
dates, equality, negative conditions, enumerations and formal defects across
grant, subsidy, procurement, social, research, education, energy, finance and
medical policies.
